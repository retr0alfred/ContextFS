# Consent form — ContextFS re-finding study

**Researchers:** Alfred Mathew, Abu Ibrahim Mothi (B.E. CSE). **Supervisor:** Dr. Murari Devakannan Kamalesh.

You are invited to help test ContextFS, a program that helps people find files again from what
they remember about them. Please read this, ask any questions, and sign only if you agree.

**What you will do (about 60 minutes):**
1. Choose a folder on your own laptop.
2. Write 8–12 short descriptions of files you would like to find again.
3. Let the program index the folder.
4. Grade the search results it shows you.

**What stays on your laptop:**
- Your files, their names and their contents.
- The program runs from a USB drive, uses no internet, and **does not change, move or delete any
  of your files**.
- Its temporary index is deleted at the end of the session, in front of you.

**What we take away:**
- Your query texts (you can edit or delete any of them first);
- your grades;
- anonymous result codes;
- timings;
- optionally, anonymous counts of file-time patterns (for example "how many PDFs were copied").
- **We never take file names, folder names, paths, contents or dates.**
- You will see the exact data on screen before it is saved, and you get a copy.

**Your rights:**
- Taking part is voluntary and unpaid.
- You may stop at any time, without giving a reason, and we will delete everything collected from
  you.
- The data is used only for this research and the resulting paper, stored on the researchers'
  machines, and deleted within 12 months.
